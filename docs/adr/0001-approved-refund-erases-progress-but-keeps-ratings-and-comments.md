# An Approved Refund Request erases Progress but keeps ratings and comments

When an Admin approves a Refund Request, the learner loses access to the course and their Progress in it (enrollment, lesson progress, video watch events, quiz attempts and answers) is permanently deleted at the moment of approval, not when the money is confirmed as sent. Their course rating and lesson comments are kept: comments are part of threads other people replied to, and we chose not to touch user-generated content as part of a refund.

## Consequences

- Erasure is irreversible, which is why approval is final and the learner is warned before submitting a Refund Request if they have any Progress.
- A Failed Refund leaves the learner without Progress or access until the team resends the money; the decision does not change.
- A refunded learner's rating still counts toward the course's score.
