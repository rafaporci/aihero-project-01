# Cadence

A course marketplace where Instructors sell courses and Students buy and watch them.

## Language

### Money back

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get the money for one of their Purchases back. It is requested, then approved or declined by staff.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Refund (for a request that has not been approved)

**Refund**:
Money from a Purchase returned to the learner after their Refund Request is approved, which also ends their access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which the learner may submit a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Chargeback**:
Reserved for a bank-initiated dispute of a card payment, which Cadence does not model yet. Never a synonym for Refund.
