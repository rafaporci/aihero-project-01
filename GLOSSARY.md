# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around refunds and a learner's progress through a course.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it, and either decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once, and none after one is approved.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet. The learner keeps access to the course while it is open.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and at that moment the learner loses access to the course and their Progress in it is erased. It cannot be undone.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down. It cannot be reopened and has no Refund, but the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

### Learning

**Progress**:
A learner's record of working through a course: lessons completed, video watched and quiz attempts. Their rating and comments are not Progress.
